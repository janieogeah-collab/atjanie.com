*Prompt: What do you love about where you live?*

Quarantine lifted, I set my sights on Xġhajra,   
Feet brushing the pavement, finally feeling free.   
Slow steps on cool tiles, a pace I couldn’t find in Naija,   
Valentine's still distant, but love flows through these narrow streets.  
  
Out of the alley, I wandered to Zabbar,   
Eyes wide, soaking in the charm,   
Lingering in awe, wrapped in euphoria,   
Every building in harmony, a perfect Maltese calm.  
  
I’d have stayed, lost in this timeless scene,   
But the sea’s call lured me to Sliema’s shore.   
Through the tunnel, the January breeze brushed clean,   
The water—a dance of blues and greens, a sight to adore.  
  
The grandeur of Mdina, a realm to explore,   
Old stone walls, regal, like the house of Welf.   
Windows creaked open, heads peeked to implore,   
Curious glances like Fgura’s, where neighbors keep to themselves.  
  
‘Xalabiebi, mhux hekk’—these words are like a savory stew,   
Not harsh, not loud, unlike the Maltese norm.   
I’m listening, I’m learning, soon I’ll rap them for you,   
So if you see me on Malta’s streets, just wink and say ‘Orrajt’?
