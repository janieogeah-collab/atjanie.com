*WordPress Prompt: Why do you blog?*

Anyone who has ever shared a bed with me calls me a nocturnal creature. The night is when my thoughts begin to marinate. I may lie still for hours, giving the illusion of deep sleep, but in truth, I’m having intense, thought-provoking conversations with myself.

There’s a whole world inside my head, an entirely different life filled with “what ifs” that explore alternate realities far more exciting than the mundane. I am an overthinker, spending about 85% of my time lost in thought, fabricating endless scenarios like: what if dreams were the real world, and waking life was the illusion? What if age were wealth, and only the old could live in luxury? What if sight were blindness, and only the blind could truly see? Or what if darkness were the source of light, and light was merely its shadow?

Blogging is how I release these overwhelming thoughts before they consume me. It is how I capture bouncy ideas. Blogging is my confessional; I step away from prying eyes and bare my soul to curious individuals like you who want all the juicy details of my deeds or perhaps misdeeds. 

It is the quiet place I go to, away from the deafening sounds of human voices and the clanging cacophony of the city. Blogging is how I lend my voice to issues that matter to me and to those who people my world. 

Blogging is my way of speaking. Because I don’t often use my voice, I let my fingers do the talking.
