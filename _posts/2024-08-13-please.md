*Prompt: What is a word you feel that too many people use?*

The word is 'please' yet not said enough,   
If said enough, then not meant with heart,   
If meant with heart, then not received as such,   
A fragile gift that fails to do its part.  
  
The word is 'please,' a prayer not often answered,   
If answered enough, then a bridge we seldom cross,   
If crossed, then not without some fear engendered,   
A step too close to risking pain or loss.   
  
The word is 'please,' a seed of quiet yearning,   
If yearned enough, then it might spark a fire,   
If sparked, then not without some slow discerning,   
A flame that warms but never climbs too high.   
  
The word is 'please,' a thread of human need,   
If needed enough, it binds us close with care,   
If bound, then not without the strength to lead,   
A gentle tether, holding love’s repair.
