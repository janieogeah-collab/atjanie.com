*WordPress Prompt: What activities do you lose yourself in?*

I’m that kind of girl who bumps into a stranger and spends way too much time creating elaborate backstories for them. Thoughts like, *” He’s definitely coming from a carnival, look at his shoes”*, always pop into my head. There’s always a story to tell about someone so yes, I lose myself fabricating them. Who knows, I may have gotten one or two right. LOL.

But this post isn’t really about my (self-proclaimed) expertise in psychoanalysis. Well, actually, scratch that, it kind of is. Mostly, though, it’s about me trying to psychoanalyse myself. I wonder if I’m like one of those therapists who gives great advice but can’t seem to figure out their own feelings.

So the other day, I had an awkward elevator ride (no surprise there) and it got me thinking…  

Am I a fraud for presenting as a communication specialist and yet cannot arrive at the best strategy for riding in an elevator?  

You see, I find elevators to be like these tiny, moving social pressure cookers.  
It feels so unnaturally awkward to be standing in close proximity to someone, especially if you ‘kinda’ know them but not well enough to ask what they had for breakfast.  

That very small space suddenly feels like the world’s largest hall and I’m on the very last seat in the back.  

What do I do when 15 seconds stretches unnaturally long?  

Make small talk?  

Stay silent and listen to their breathing?  

Pretend to be checking my phone?  

And when they accidentally make eye contact, do I acknowledge it? Nod? Smile? Immediately look away?  

Believe me, the tension in this tiny space fills me with dread, and it makes solo rides feel like luxury.  

My mind never raced so much with these little calculations when preparing communication plans.  

Perhaps I’m like that therapist after all.  

So, what do I lose myself in? Overthinking, fabricating stories, and the endless social calculations of everyday life. Whether it’s psychoanalysing strangers or surviving an elevator ride, my mind never stops spinning. Maybe that’s just who I am, an overthinker, or maybe I’m just a busybody, LOL.
