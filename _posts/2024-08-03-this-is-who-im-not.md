I’m not boring; I just prefer staying indoors,   
I’m not snobbish; my mind seeks more than what's yours,   
I don’t dislike people; I keep to myself, not in scores,   
I’m not quiet; I grasp the unspoken with no roars.   
  
I’m not rude; I struggle to relate and explore,   
I’m not antisocial; content at home’s where I soar,   
I’m not shy; deeper bonds help me open the doors,   
I’m not a pessimist; I overthink to prepare for the wars.   
  
I’m smarter than I seem, though I babble and implore,   
So I’m not who you think; I have many sides to adore.
